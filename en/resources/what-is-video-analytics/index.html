What Is Video Analytics? From Motion Detection to AI Event DetectionWhat video analytics is, how AI-based analytics differs from motion detection, typical events, where it is used in factories, warehouses, retail and banking, and its limitations.
2026-10-03

A plain-language explanation of video analytics and what changed with deep learning.

Video analytics is software that automatically analyses camera footage and produces events, counts and metrics, so that important situations are noticed as they happen rather than found later in recordings.

ContactBook a demo

Motion detection vs AI analytics

Older analytics mostly reacted to pixel changes: moving trees, shadows and lighting changes all triggered alerts. AI-based analytics recognises what is in the image — a person, a forklift, smoke — so rules can be meaningful (“a person in this zone after 22:00”) and false alarms drop.

Typical events

  • Zone entry, dwell time and line crossing
  • Counting people, vehicles or objects
  • Missing PPE, falls, fire and smoke
  • Queue length and occupancy

Where it is used

In factories for workplace safety and production monitoring ; in warehouses for forklift safety and counting; in retail and banking for people counting and queue analytics .

Limitations

Video analytics only sees what the camera sees. Angle, distance, lighting and occlusion set the limits, so each project should include camera evaluation and accuracy measurement on real footage.
